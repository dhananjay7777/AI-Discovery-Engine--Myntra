# Phase 7 Eval — Research Handoff

<!-- phase-status: not_started -->

**Phase goal.** The engine's output becomes the visible, traceable input to primary research.

**Why this phase is gated.** The brief judges whether thinking evolved from business metric through discovery to problem definition. If the interview guide could have been written without the engine, the engine did not matter.

---

## Tests

| ID | Test | Method | Pass condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T7.1 | Target selected | Inspect the decision log | One opportunity and one segment chosen, with written rationale referencing engine output; `O-01` closed |
| T7.2 | Selection is defensible | Review against Phase 4 output | Choice follows from score, confidence, and non-monetary actionability — or the deviation is explicitly justified |
| T7.3 | Guide is engine-derived | Trace each question to its source | Every question maps to a specific open question, code, or piece of evidence |
| T7.4 | Guide could not be written blind | Human review | ≥ 60% of questions reference a specific behavior or workaround surfaced by the corpus, not generic wishlist curiosity |
| T7.5 | Non-leading questions | Review all questions | No question presupposes the hypothesis; behavior-first, past-tense phrasing |
| T7.6 | Covers the brief's research asks | Checklist against problem statement Part 3 | Why saved, still intend, what stops them, what would unblock, information needed, alternatives considered, off-app behavior, current coping |
| T7.7 | Screener derived from segments | Review screener | Criteria trace to the opportunity's segment signals; screens out the wrong segment |
| T7.8 | Falsifiability | Review the guide | States in advance what answers would *kill* the hypothesis (`D-014`) |
| T7.9 | Evidence export | Run the export | Quotes with platform, date, permalink, code, and opportunity; opens cleanly |
| T7.10 | One-slide artifact | Review the slide | Sources → processing → ranking → UI, understandable in 60 seconds by someone who has not seen the site |
| T7.11 | Narrative chain | Review the write-up | Business metric → product outcomes → AI discovery → open questions is traceable end to end |
| T7.12 | Constraint held | Review the guide and framing | No monetary-incentive question or premise anywhere |
| T7.13 | Interview length realistic | Dry-run the guide once | Fits 30–40 minutes without rushing; trimmed if not |
| T7.14 | Limitations carried forward | Review the handoff note | States what the engine could not see, so interviews cover those gaps deliberately |

---

## Exit criteria

1. Target opportunity and segment selected with engine-traceable rationale; `O-01` closed (T7.1, T7.2).
2. Every interview question traces to engine output; ≥ 60% reference corpus-specific behavior (T7.3, T7.4).
3. Questions are non-leading and cover all of the brief's Part 3 asks (T7.5, T7.6).
4. Screener derived from segment signals (T7.7).
5. Falsification conditions written before interviews begin (T7.8).
6. Evidence export and the one-slide artifact both produced (T7.9, T7.10).
7. The full narrative chain is traceable (T7.11).
8. No monetary premise anywhere (T7.12).

---

## Known failure modes

| Failure mode | Detection | Response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Interview guide is generic and the engine was decoration | T7.4 | Rewrite each question from a specific quote or workaround in the corpus |
| Questions lead the witness toward the chosen hypothesis | T7.5 | Convert to behavior-first: "walk me through the last thing you saved and didn't buy" |
| Hypothesis is unfalsifiable, so interviews can only confirm it | T7.8 | Write kill criteria before the first interview |
| Chosen segment cannot actually be recruited | T7.7, T7.13 | Check recruitability before locking the segment |
| Engine limitations forgotten, so interviews inherit the same blind spots | T7.14 | Add questions specifically targeting what public conversation cannot reveal |
| Deck claims the engine proved the problem | T7.11 | Frame as narrowed field plus validated-by-research; the engine ranks, interviews decide |

---

## Artifacts to keep

Selection rationale, interview guide with per-question provenance, screener, falsification criteria, evidence export file, the one-slide diagram, and the handoff note listing carried-forward limitations.
